Operational runbooks for common procedures.

This section contains step-by-step procedures for provisioning, maintenance, troubleshooting, and recovery.

**Use this section for:** provisioning procedures, maintenance checklists, troubleshooting guides, recovery procedures.

**Do not use this section for:** high-level policies (use Operations), architecture (use Architecture), service definitions (use Services).

**Archetype:** `runbook` for all runbook pages, `default` for overview pages.

**Relates to:** Operations (policies and schedules), Services (services being recovered or maintained).
